## What to look for

- **Drag a handle.** The Unovis `Brush` sits in a small chart of its own and sets the main chart's `xDomain`;
  `scaleByDomain` fits the y axis to the selected days. Recharts' `<Brush>` and ECharts' `dataZoom` slider live inside
  the chart and fit the y axis too. In Vega-Lite, an interval selection on an overview view sets the x domain of the
  main view, as in its overview + detail example; the y axis keeps the whole year's range. TanStack Charts' `brushX`
  reports a range, and the demo filters the rows and sets the x domain itself.
- **Move the range.** Unovis moves a range only by its handles unless you turn on `draggable`, which the *30-day window*
  preset does. In the other libraries, dragging inside the selection moves it by default.
- **Start without a selection.** The Unovis, Recharts, ECharts and TanStack brushes start on the whole year with a
  handle at each end; Vega-Lite starts empty, and you drag out a range on the overview.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis keeps the selected days and animates the chart. Recharts draws its brush across the
  whole year again while the chart stays on the selected days.
- **Chart.js, Nivo, AG Charts and Observable Plot.** Chart.js, Nivo and Plot have no brush (Chart.js' official zoom
  plugin can drag a zoom box), and AG Charts' Navigator is an Enterprise feature.
- **Presets.** *30-day window* (`selection`, `draggable`) is one every library here can draw: Recharts takes data
  indices, the others dates. *Outside handles* (`handlePosition`, `handleWidth`) sets 14-pixel handles: Recharts
  (`travellerWidth`) and TanStack Charts (`handleSize`, 24 pixels by default) set the width, ECharts takes a handle icon
  whose shape gives the width, and none of them has an option for where the handles sit. Vega-Lite's interval selection
  has no handles. *Zoom box* (`VisFreeBrush` with `mode: xy`) zooms both axes into a rectangle you drag on the chart;
  only ECharts has it built in, as the toolbox's `dataZoom` feature, which the demo switches on with a
  `takeGlobalCursor` action; Chart.js gets it from its official chartjs-plugin-zoom (`drag.enabled` with `mode: 'xy'`).
  Recharts' docs build one from mouse events and a `ReferenceArea`, TanStack Charts brushes and zooms the x axis only,
  and a Vega-Lite rectangle selection sets the domains of another view, not its own.
