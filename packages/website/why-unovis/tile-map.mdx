# Tile Map

Magnitude 4.5+ earthquakes from one month of the [USGS earthquake feed](https://earthquake.usgs.gov/earthquakes/feed/),
clustered on a vector tile map, colored by magnitude class and sized by magnitude. Unovis draws it with
[LeafletMap](/docs/components/LeafletMap). None of the other libraries has a tile map with clustering; the official ECharts
extension draws on Baidu Maps only.

</LibraryComparison>

## What to look for

- **Clusters that show what's inside.** Unovis draws every cluster as a small donut split by magnitude class
  (`colorMap`), so you can spot the strong earthquakes before you zoom in.
- **Dark mode.** Unovis switches to its `styleDarkTheme` basemap when the page does.
- **Tooltips.** One Tooltip with triggers covers single points and clusters.
- **Legend.** The legend hides and shows magnitude classes.
- **Zoom and pan.** Scroll to zoom smoothly on the vector tiles and click a cluster to expand it.
