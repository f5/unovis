# Treemap

Monthly cloud spend of five teams, split into the services each team runs: every tile's area is the service's cost.

</LibraryComparison>

## What to look for

- **Labels.** Unovis wraps each label inside its tile, sizes the text by the tile's value
  (`enableTileLabelFontSizeVariation`, on by default) and skips tiles under 20px (`minTileSizeForLabel`). TanStack
  Charts draws a label only where it fits. ECharts shortens labels with an ellipsis, Chart.js cuts them at the tile's
  edge, and Nivo turns them sideways in tall tiles (`orientLabel`) and lets them run past small ones. Recharts' default
  content shows names only and writes each team's name in the middle of its group, under the service names.
- **Team colors.** Unovis draws every service in a lighter shade of its team's palette color. TanStack Charts and
  ECharts give a team's services one color, Recharts fills the teams and draws the services transparent on top of them,
  and Nivo draws every node at a third of its opacity, so the services get darker where they overlap their team. The
  Chart.js treemap plugin has no default colors, so the demo takes the Chart.js palette.
- **Hide a team.** Unovis' legend (a `BulletLegend` with a few lines of state, plus `tileColor` so the teams keep their
  colors) and TanStack Charts' interactive legend remove the team, and the others fill the space. Recharts' and Nivo's
  treemaps have no legend, and the ECharts and Chart.js legends list series and datasets, not the groups of a treemap.
- **Click a tile.** ECharts zooms into it by default (`nodeClick: 'zoomToNode'`) and shows a breadcrumb to go back up;
  Recharts can drill down with `type="nest"`. Unovis has no drill-down.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis, TanStack Charts, ECharts and Nivo move and resize the tiles. Recharts keys its nodes
  by position and Chart.js grows every tile in again, so both replay their entry animation.
- **Presets.** *Group labels* (`labelInternalNodes`, `tilePaddingTop`) are Chart.js' `captions`, ECharts'
  `upperLabel` and Nivo's parent labels; TanStack Charts renders only the leaves and Recharts has no header option.
  *Rounded tiles* (`tilePadding`, `tileBorderRadius`) are a pair of options in TanStack Charts, Chart.js and ECharts;
  Recharts and Nivo have the gaps but no corner radius. *Shaded tiles* (`enableLightnessVariance`) shades each team's
  services by cost; only ECharts can do the same, with a level's `colorSaturation` range.
