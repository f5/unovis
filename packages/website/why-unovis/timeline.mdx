## What to look for

- **Lanes.** Unovis takes each item's start, duration and lane (`x`, `lineDuration`, `lineRow`) and draws the lanes with
  labels and alternating backgrounds. The other libraries draw floating bars on a band axis: Recharts, following its
  timeline example, needs one row per lane and one `Bar` per item in it, ECharts draws every item in a custom series'
  `renderItem` as in its Gantt example, and Chart.js needs `grouped: false` so the bars of different kinds share a lane.
- **Time axis.** Chart.js' time scale needs a separate date adapter package, so its demo uses a linear scale with a step
  of one day. TanStack Charts has no time scale of its own and takes d3-scale's `scaleTime`, as its docs recommend.
- **Hover.** Every library shows the item's title, service, kind and time. Recharts' default tooltip loses its heading
  with `shared={false}`, so the demo renders a custom `content` in the default style.
- **Hide a kind.** Unovis, Recharts, Chart.js, ECharts, Observable Plot and TanStack Charts hide it; Vega-Lite's legend
  highlights a kind instead. The added marks don't follow every legend: Chart.js annotations check the dataset's
  visibility themselves, and TanStack Charts' legend hides only the rectangles, so the demo filters the arrows.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis moves every item to its new place, matched by `id`. Observable Plot and Vega-Lite redraw
  without a transition.
- **Presets.** *Rounded* (`lineCap`) is one every library can draw; TanStack Charts draws the short items as ovals.
  *Arrows* (`arrows`) are elbows in Unovis, down from the cause and along the incident's lane into it. Chart.js (with the official annotation plugin), ECharts (a `lines` series), Observable Plot and TanStack
  Charts (`arrow` marks) draw straight arrows; Recharts and Vega-Lite have no arrowheads. *Icons* (`lineStartIcon`,
  `lineEndIcon`) are SVG symbols in Unovis. ECharts and Vega-Lite take the same SVG path as a symbol, Chart.js (a point
  annotation) and Observable Plot (an `image` mark) take it as an image in a hard-coded color, and Recharts and
  TanStack Charts have no icon or custom symbol option.
