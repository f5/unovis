## What to look for

- **Labels.** Unovis puts the hours and days around the grid with `columnLabel` and `rowLabel`, and hides the labels
  that would overlap (`labelHideOverlapping`, on by default). ECharts, Chart.js and TanStack Charts skip labels too, and
  Chart.js turns its hour labels at an angle. Vega-Lite turns them sideways, and Nivo and Observable Plot draw every
  label, so they crowd at this width.
- **Missing hours.** Unovis draws a cell without a value in its empty color (`--vis-heatmap-cell-fill-color`), so the
  outage reads as no data. ECharts (`'-'` values), Vega-Lite and Observable Plot leave a hole. Nivo paints empty cells
  black unless you set `emptyColor`, and TanStack Charts paints a null value in the scale's first color, the same as the
  lowest rates, so its demo leaves those rows out, as the Chart.js demo does.
- **One color scale.** Every library gets the same four steps, Unovis' default heatmap colors, over 0–4%
  (`colorDomain`). In Unovis they're CSS variables, so the cells and the legend switch to the dark steps without any
  code; the other demos pick the dark colors from `isDark`.
- **Legends.** Unovis has no color legend on the heatmap itself; the demo puts a `BulletLegend` with the bucket colors
  above it. Chart.js' legend lists datasets, so its buckets are custom legend items. ECharts draws the scale as a
  piecewise `visualMap`, which hides a bucket's cells when you click it.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis, Chart.js and Nivo fade the cells to their new colors. TanStack Charts, Observable Plot
  and Vega-Lite switch them at once. On hover, Nivo dims every cell outside the hovered row and column.
- **Presets.** *Rounded gaps* (`cellPadding`, `cellCornerRadius`) are options in every library, in different units:
  Nivo and Vega-Lite take the gap as a share of the band, Observable Plot and TanStack Charts as an `inset`, and
  Chart.js as a cell size you compute from the chart area. ECharts has no gap option for heatmap cells, so its demo
  draws a border in the card's background color. *Eight buckets* (`colorRange`) is a list of colors everywhere; the
  Unovis and Chart.js legends wrap to a second row, while ECharts' `visualMap` stays on one row and cuts off its first
  and last buckets. *Hours as rows* (`layout`) fills the grid row by row from the same flat list in Unovis; the other
  libraries swap their x and y fields.
