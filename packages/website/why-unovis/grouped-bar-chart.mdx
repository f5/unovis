# Grouped Bar Chart

Annual revenue by region for three years, as grouped bars. The region names are long on purpose.

<LibraryComparison comparison={groupedBarChart}/>

## What to look for

- **Long labels.** Unovis wraps "Middle East & Africa" onto two lines within the space of its group. Recharts and
  ECharts hide labels that don't fit, Chart.js rotates them and Nivo lets them overlap. AG Charts wraps them too.
- **Hover.** The Unovis crosshair shows all three years of a region in one tooltip, wherever you point within the group.
  Nivo shows one bar at a time.
- **Randomize the data.** Bars grow and shrink from their current height. AG Charts Community redraws without a
  transition.
- **Rounded corners.** Unovis rounds bar corners by default, which keeps dense bar charts soft on the eye.
