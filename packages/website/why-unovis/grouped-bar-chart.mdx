# Grouped Bar Chart

Annual revenue by region for three years, as grouped bars. The region names are long on purpose.

<LibraryComparison comparison={groupedBarChart}/>

## What to look for

- **Long labels.** Unovis wraps "Middle East & Africa" onto two lines within the space of its group. Recharts and
  ECharts hide labels that don't fit, Chart.js rotates them and Nivo lets them overlap. AG Charts wraps them too.
  Observable Plot centers each name under its group and lets long ones overlap; Vega-Lite turns them vertical, which
  takes about a third of the chart's height.
- **Hover.** The Unovis crosshair shows all three years of a region in one tooltip, wherever you point within the group.
  Nivo shows one bar at a time. Observable Plot (`tip: 'x'`) and Vega-Lite (an extra, invisible layer per region) can
  show the whole group too.
- **TanStack Charts.** Groups the bars with `layout: group()` and shows the whole region in one tooltip
  (`focus: 'group-x'`). Its legend hides a year, but the empty slot in each group stays and the y axis keeps its range.
- **Randomize the data.** Bars grow and shrink from their current height. AG Charts Community, Observable Plot and
  Vega-Lite redraw without a transition.
- **Rounded corners.** Unovis rounds bar corners by default, which keeps dense bar charts soft on the eye.
