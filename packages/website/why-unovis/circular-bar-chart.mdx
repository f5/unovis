# Circular Bar Chart

Today's progress towards six daily health goals: one bar per goal, growing outward from the middle, over a faded track
that shows the goal. Every goal has its own target, so each bar is scaled to its own maximum.

</LibraryComparison>

## What to look for

- **One bar, one goal.** Unovis scales each bar to its own target (`maxValue` takes an accessor), so steps and sleep
  share a chart without a shared unit. The other libraries draw every bar on one radial scale, so their data is
  converted to percent of the goal first; open **Show the data** and **Code** to see where. Nivo then stretches the
  longest bar to the outer radius, so its chart only shows the goals relative to each other.
- **The shape.** The Unovis bars are petals: parallel sides with a constant gap, rounded outer corners and a rounded
  tip, all derived from the chart radius so the proportions hold at any size, over a faded track that shows the goal.
  ECharts draws rounded arcs over a track. Recharts draws them with an undocumented `layout="centric"` of its
  `RadialBarChart`, without a track: its `background` is only drawn in the ring layout. Chart.js draws wedges from the
  center, with rounded corners and a small gap but no hole and no track. Nivo draws rounded arcs with no gap between
  them and no track. Vega-Lite has no polar bar chart, but its `arc` mark draws one, with a track, once the start and
  end angle of every bar are computed in the data; open **Code** to see how. TanStack Charts draws it from its `polar`
  module (`radialBarRadius` on a band angle scale, with a track in a separate layer). Observable Plot has no arc mark or
  polar coordinates.
- **Icons.** Unovis places an icon at the inner end of each bar (`icon`, an SVG symbol or an icon-font glyph) and hides
  it when the bar is too short. None of the other libraries has an option for it.
- **The legend.** Unovis, Recharts and Chart.js list the bars and hide one when you click it (Unovis and Recharts
  with a few lines of state); the remaining Unovis bars spread out to fill the circle. Vega-Lite's legend highlights the
  bar you click and fades the others. TanStack Charts' legend doesn't hide polar marks itself, so the demo filters the
  bars. ECharts' legend lists series and Nivo's lists keys, so a single series of bars
  gets no legend in either.
- **Randomize the data.** Bar lengths animate to the new values in Unovis, Chart.js, ECharts and Nivo. Recharts
  animates angles only, so in this layout the bars jump to their new lengths, and Vega-Lite redraws without a
  transition.
- **Switch the site theme.** The faded tracks and icons follow the theme through CSS variables
  (`--vis-circular-bar-background-color`, `--vis-circular-bar-icon-color`); the other charts need their dark colors in code.
