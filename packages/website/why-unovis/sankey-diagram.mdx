## What to look for

- **Labels.** Unovis labels every node on its inner side (`labelPosition`), adds its value underneath (`subLabel`) and
  trims or wraps labels that don't fit. Recharts draws no labels at all; the code adds them with a custom node, the way
  its docs do.
- **Hover.** Unovis shows the value of the flow under the pointer and, after a short delay, highlights every path
  through the node it leads to (`highlightSubtreeOnHover`). ECharts highlights the nodes and flows next to the one you
  hover.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis moves nodes and flows to their new sizes in one transition.
- **Colors.** Unovis draws nodes in one color and keeps the flows neutral, so the structure reads first; `nodeColor` and
  `linkColor` color them when you need to. ECharts and Nivo color every node, and the Chart.js sankey plugin colors
  flows red to green unless you set colors yourself.
