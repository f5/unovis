## What to look for

- **The label in the middle.** Unovis and AG Charts have options for it. Recharts places a `Label` and ECharts a title.
  Chart.js needs a plugin: the official annotation plugin's `doughnutLabel`, or a few lines of custom plugin as here.
  Nivo needs a custom layer, Vega-Lite a text layer with its own one-row data, and TanStack Charts two `radialText`
  marks in a separate, `decorative()` polar layer. Open **Code** to compare.
- **Hide a source.** The Unovis segments sweep closed and the others grow to fill the ring. Vega-Lite's legend
  highlights a source and fades the others instead. TanStack Charts' legend doesn't hide polar marks itself, so the demo
  zeroes the hidden source and the other slices close the ring, as in Unovis.
- **Randomize the data.** Segments animate to their new angles; TanStack Charts tweens the slice shapes
  (`svgAnimation`). AG Charts Community and Vega-Lite redraw without a transition.
- **Styling.** The ring width, the gaps between segments and rounded corners are single options in Unovis
  (`arcWidth`, `padAngle`, `cornerRadius`), and colors come from CSS variables.
- **Presets.** *Rounded gaps* (`padAngle`, `cornerRadius`) is a pair of options in every library. *Half donut*
  (`angleRange`) also recenters the ring in Unovis and moves the label under it; Chart.js and Nivo fit the half ring to
  the card too; Recharts, ECharts and Vega-Lite need a hand-set center and radius; TanStack Charts has no center option
  and needs a locked top `margin` and a hand-set radius; and the AG Charts donut has no start or end angle. *Patterns*
  (`pattern`) gives every segment a built-in fill pattern, as ECharts' decals, Nivo's `defs` and AG Charts' pattern
  fills do; Recharts, Chart.js, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts have none.
