# Line Chart

Monthly active users on three platforms over three years, with a few months of iOS and Android data missing. Every
chart gets the same data, and each library is set up to draw the same chart: lines without point markers, formatted
axes, a legend that toggles series and a tooltip on hover.

## What to look for

- **Hover.** The Unovis crosshair snaps to the nearest month, marks every line and shows one tooltip for all three.
  Recharts and ECharts do the same. Chart.js shows a shared tooltip without a guide line, Nivo draws the line without
  marking the points, and AG Charts Community shows a shared tooltip without a guide line, because its crosshair is an
  Enterprise feature. Observable Plot and Vega-Lite show one tooltip per month with a guide line, without marking the
  points: Plot with `Plot.tip` and `pointerX`, Vega-Lite with a pivot transform and a hover rule. TanStack Charts shows one
  tooltip per month with a crosshair (`focus: 'group-x'`), leaving out a series that has no data that month.
- **Gaps in the data.** Unovis bridges the missing months with a dashed line from one option,
  `interpolateMissingData`, so the trend stays readable and it's clear which months have no data. Chart.js draws the
  same dashed bridges with `spanGaps` and a segment style. Recharts, ECharts and AG Charts can only bridge gaps with the
  same solid line, which hides that data is missing, so they leave gaps here, and Nivo can't bridge them at all.
  Observable Plot, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts leave gaps by default too.
  Randomize the data to move the gaps.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis morphs the lines and the axis ticks to the new values. AG Charts Community redraws
  without a transition, because animations are an Enterprise feature, and so do Observable Plot and Vega-Lite, which
  have no animations. TanStack Charts tweens the lines (`svgAnimation`), but only those without gaps; the others jump.
- **Click the legend.** Hidden lines disappear and the y axis rescales to what's left. Unovis and Recharts need a few
  lines of state for this; Chart.js, ECharts and AG Charts do it on their own, and Nivo with its `toggleSerie` option.
  Observable Plot's legend is static, so it takes the same few lines of state. Vega-Lite's legend highlights the line you
  click and fades the others, so the axis doesn't rescale. TanStack Charts has its own clickable legend, but a hidden
  line keeps its room on the y axis.
- **Switch the site theme.** Unovis follows it through CSS, Observable Plot's text and axes follow it through
  `currentColor`, and so does TanStack Charts' whole default theme. Every other chart gets its dark colors or theme from code; open **Code** to see how much.
- **The x axis.** Unovis spaces its ticks by the width available, so labels never collide. Chart.js rotates the month
  labels and skips every other one to fit them.
