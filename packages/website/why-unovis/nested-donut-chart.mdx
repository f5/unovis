## What to look for

- **The data goes in as a table.** Unovis takes the rows and one accessor per ring (`layers`), and TanStack Charts a
  path per row. Recharts, ECharts and Nivo take a tree, so their demos build one first; Recharts also needs a value on
  every node, the root too, and Nivo and Recharts need unique ids or names, which the repeated browser names aren't.
  Chart.js has no hierarchical chart and the AG Charts Sunburst series is Enterprise-only: each ring is a doughnut
  dataset (Chart.js) or donut series (AG Charts) of its own that lines up with the others because the demo adds up each
  ring in the same order. Vega-Lite has no hierarchy layout either: every ring is a layer that
  sums its groups and runs a cumulative sum for the angles. Open **Code** to compare.
- **Segment labels.** Unovis labels every segment by default and hides the labels that don't fit
  (`hideOverflowingSegmentLabels`). ECharts labels every segment too, small ones included, unless `label.minAngle` sets
  the narrowest segment that gets a label. Nivo's arc labels are off by default and `arcLabelsSkipAngle` hides them on
  narrow segments. Recharts always labels the value, not the name. AG Charts draws a donut's `sectorLabel` on every
  segment and hides it where it doesn't fit. Chart.js needs chartjs-plugin-datalabels and Vega-Lite a text layer per
  ring, and the TanStack Charts sunburst mark has no labels (its polar `radialText` mark could add them at angles the
  app computes).
- **Colors.** Unovis colors each platform and draws its operating systems and browsers in lighter shades of it. ECharts,
  Nivo and TanStack Charts (`color: 'branchId'`) give the children their parent's color. Recharts has no palette, so the
  platforms get colors from its docs; Chart.js and AG Charts would give every segment of every ring a color of its own,
  so the demos color them by platform.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis, Chart.js, ECharts and Nivo animate the segments to their new angles, and TanStack
  Charts tweens them (`svgAnimation`). Recharts' sunburst, Vega-Lite and AG Charts
  (animations are Enterprise) redraw without a transition.
- **Drill down.** Clicking a segment in ECharts zooms into it, on by default (`nodeClick`). TanStack Charts has a
  `rootId` option for it, with the navigation state in your app. Unovis has no drill-down.
- **Presets.** *Inwards* (`direction`) puts the platforms on the outer ring, which is Unovis' default; TanStack Charts
  swaps its inner and outer radius, Chart.js its datasets, AG Charts its series' radius ratios, and ECharts and
  Vega-Lite set every ring's radius, while Recharts and Nivo always draw from the center out. *Half circle*
  (`angleRange`) works in Recharts, Chart.js, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts; the ECharts sunburst and AG Charts' donut
  series have a start angle (`rotation` in AG Charts) but no end angle, and Nivo's sunburst has neither. *Ring widths*
  (`layerSettings`) gives every ring its own width, like ECharts' `levels`, Chart.js' `weight`, AG Charts'
  `innerRadiusRatio` and `outerRadiusRatio` and Vega-Lite's radii; Recharts, Nivo and TanStack Charts divide the radius
  into rings themselves.
