## What to look for

- **The data goes in as a table.** Unovis takes the rows and one accessor per ring (`layers`), and TanStack Charts a
  path per row. Recharts, ECharts and Nivo take a tree, so their demos build one first; Recharts also needs a value on
  every node, the root too, and Nivo and Recharts need unique ids or names, which the repeated browser names aren't.
  Chart.js has no hierarchical chart: each ring is a doughnut dataset of its own that lines up with the others because
  the demo adds up each ring in the same order. Vega-Lite has no hierarchy layout either: every ring is a layer that
  sums its groups and runs a cumulative sum for the angles. Open **Code** to compare.
- **Segment labels.** Unovis labels every segment by default and hides the labels that don't fit
  (`hideOverflowingSegmentLabels`). ECharts labels every segment too, small ones included. Nivo's arc labels are off by
  default and `arcLabelsSkipAngle` hides them on narrow segments. Recharts always labels the value, not the name.
  Chart.js needs chartjs-plugin-datalabels and Vega-Lite a text layer per ring, and the TanStack Charts sunburst mark has
  no labels.
- **Colors.** Unovis colors each platform and draws its operating systems and browsers in lighter shades of it. ECharts,
  Nivo and TanStack Charts (`color: 'branchId'`) give the children their parent's color. Recharts has no palette, so the
  platforms get colors from its docs; Chart.js would give every segment of every ring a color of its own, so the demo
  colors them by platform.
- **Randomize the data.** Unovis, Chart.js, ECharts and Nivo animate the segments to their new angles, and TanStack
  Charts tweens them (`svgAnimation`). Recharts' sunburst and Vega-Lite redraw without a transition.
- **Drill down.** Clicking a segment in ECharts zooms into it, on by default (`nodeClick`). TanStack Charts has a
  `rootId` option for it, with the navigation state in your app. Unovis has no drill-down.
- **Presets.** *Inwards* (`direction`) puts the platforms on the outer ring, which is Unovis' default; TanStack Charts
  swaps its inner and outer radius, Chart.js its datasets, and ECharts and Vega-Lite set every ring's radius, while
  Recharts and Nivo always draw from the center out. *Half circle* (`angleRange`) works in Recharts, Chart.js,
  Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts; the ECharts sunburst has a start angle but no end angle, and Nivo's has neither. *Ring
  widths* (`layerSettings`) gives every ring its own width, like ECharts' `levels`, Chart.js' `weight` and Vega-Lite's
  radii; Recharts, Nivo and TanStack Charts divide the radius into rings themselves.
