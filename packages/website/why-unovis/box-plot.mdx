## What to look for

- **What goes in.** The Unovis `Boxplot` draws the statistics you give it (`median`, `quartiles` and `whiskers`), so
  the demo computes them from the samples with the helper in `data.ts`, and the boxes can show any summary you like.
  Recharts has no box plot: its docs example draws one from the same precomputed statistics, with a ranged bar, an
  error bar and a scatter. Chart.js (with the @sgratzl/chartjs-chart-boxplot plugin), ECharts (with its `boxplot`
  dataset transform), Nivo, Observable Plot, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts compute the statistics from the raw samples.
- **Whiskers.** Unovis, Recharts, Chart.js, Observable Plot, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts end the whiskers at the
  last sample within 1.5 × IQR of the box. ECharts ends them at the 1.5 × IQR bound itself, so some of its whiskers are
  longer than the data they cover. Nivo's whiskers are fixed quantiles, the 10th and 90th percentiles by default, and
  it draws no outliers.
- **Outliers.** The Unovis `Boxplot` doesn't draw outliers: a `Scatter` in the same container does. ECharts and
  Recharts draw them as a separate scatter series too; Chart.js, Observable Plot, Vega-Lite and TanStack Charts draw
  them as part of the box plot.
- **Hover.** The Unovis crosshair shows a box's statistics anywhere in its column. Chart.js, ECharts, Nivo, Vega-Lite
  and TanStack Charts show them over the box itself. Vega-Lite's box tooltip lists the min and max of all the samples,
  outliers included, and the whiskers have a tooltip of their own. Observable Plot's `boxY` would open one tip per part
  of the box, each with a single value, so its demo adds one `Plot.tip` per box with the statistics from the
  `data.ts` helper.
- **Presets.** *Colors* (`color`) is one every library but TanStack Charts can draw: its `boxY` takes a single fill for
  all the boxes. The ECharts and Recharts demos keep the outliers in one color: they're a separate series, so coloring
  them per box takes a color callback (ECharts' `itemStyle.color`) or a `Cell` per outlier (Recharts). *Min–max
  whiskers* (`whiskers`) takes `extent: 'min-max'` in Vega-Lite, `boundIQR: 'none'` in ECharts, `coef: 0` in the
  Chart.js plugin and quantiles of 0 and 1 in Nivo; Observable Plot and TanStack Charts always draw Tukey whiskers.
  *Rounded boxes* (`roundedCorners`) works in Recharts, Nivo, Observable Plot (`rx`) and Vega-Lite (`cornerRadius`); the
  Chart.js plugin, ECharts and TanStack Charts have no corner radius for boxes.
