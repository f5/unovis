## What to look for

- **Stacking.** In Unovis, passing several `y` accessors to an Area stacks them. Chart.js needs the Filler plugin and a
  fill target on every dataset, and its stacked y axis doesn't start at zero unless you set `beginAtZero`.
- **Hide a service.** The Unovis areas above it slide down while the axis rescales, in one transition.
- **Randomize the data.** Watch the stacks morph. AG Charts Community has no animations; they're an Enterprise feature.
- **Blending.** Nivo draws each area from the baseline at 20% opacity by default, so the colors mix where they overlap.
- **Observable Plot and Vega-Lite.** Both stack areas on their own once the data has one row per month and service.
  Plot's legend is static, so hiding a service takes a few lines of state; Vega-Lite's legend highlights a service
  instead of hiding it and stacks the first legend item on top. Neither animates updates.
- **TanStack Charts.** Stacks the areas on its own, animates updates (`svgAnimation`) and has a clickable legend, but
  hiding a service removes its layer without restacking, so a gap is left and the y axis keeps its range.
- **Presets.** *Step areas* switches `curveType`, which every library can match. *Patterns* gives each service a
  built-in fill pattern through the `pattern` accessor; ECharts (decals), Nivo and AG Charts have built-in patterns too,
  while TanStack Charts, Recharts, Chart.js, Observable Plot and Vega-Lite don't. *Annotations* adds a `VisPlotband`
  and a `VisPlotline`: Chart.js needs its separate annotation plugin, Nivo has no band, and TanStack's `rect` can't span
  the plot, so its band stops at the highest total.
- **Stream graphs.** The Unovis `baseline` accessor sets a baseline for every data point, which is all a stream graph
  needs. See the [Area with Baseline](/gallery/view?title=Area%20with%20Baseline) example.
